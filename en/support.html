Frequently asked questions

Are my documents sent to a server?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. A document only leaves the device if you share it, copy it to iCloud Drive or back it up yourself. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

What is Retouche PDF Pro?

A paid option that unlocks, with no limit, the tools that really rewrite or convert a PDF: Edit a PDF, Translate PDF, PDF to Word, PDF to Excel, Compare 2 Versions, Expenses and the Integrity Seal (only sealing a document; checking a seal is free). Everything else is free: scanning, the library, filling and signing, redacting, password protection, merging, splitting, compressing, sharing and backing up.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes, in two ways. Each Pro tool can be used 10 times for free. A use is only counted when the tool gives you its result: PDF saved or shared, expenses exported, comparison shown, seal applied. Opening a tool, canceling or a failure costs nothing. The monthly and yearly subscriptions also start with a 7-day free trial, once per Apple Account. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store, in your local currency.

How do I avoid being charged when the trial ends?

A subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To cancel it: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Retouche PDF and tap Cancel Subscription. Retouche PDF Pro stays active until the end of the period already started.

I have a new iPhone: how do I get my purchase back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app’s Settings and, in the “Retouche PDF Pro” section, tap “Restore Purchases”.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are collected by Apple, which decides on refunds. Make your request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is a document “shared as images”?

When the original PDF shows exactly what the pages show, the app shares that real PDF, which you can still edit elsewhere. If a page was redacted, cropped, rotated or touched up in the app, the original PDF would show what you changed or hid: the app then shares images of the pages, and tells you first.

Does the edited text keep the document’s font?

Yes, when possible: the app rewrites the text in the file with the document’s font. If a letter is missing from that font, the app tells you and offers solutions; it never changes the font or the size without your agreement. On a scanned page, which has no font, the text is rewritten over the image, and the app warns you.

Which languages does the app read?

The app is in English and French. Text recognition reads English and French by default; you can choose other languages in the app’s Settings (OCR Languages). Receipts, their amounts and their dates are read in English and French.

How do I back up my library or move to another device?

In the app’s Settings, “Backup & Restore” section: “Back Up Library” creates a ZIP archive you can keep wherever you like; “Restore a Backup” reads it on another device. The “Copy to iCloud Drive” button (“iCloud Drive” section) also places a PDF of each document in your iCloud Drive; it does not sync your library between devices.

How do I protect my documents?

Turn on “Face ID Lock” in the app’s Settings, and keep sensitive documents in a private folder. Documents in private folders are not copied to iCloud Drive.
