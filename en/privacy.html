Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short

  • We don’t collect any data. Retouche PDF has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracker and no server.
  • Your documents stay on your device. They only leave it if you share, copy or back them up yourself.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive neither your payment details nor your identity.

1. Who publishes the app

Retouche PDF is published by Antonin. For any question about this policy: retouchepdf@gmail.com.

2. What the app does on your device

All of this processing happens on your iPhone or iPad. The results stay in the app, on your device.

  • Scanning: the camera is used to photograph your documents.
  • Text recognition: the text of your pages is read by Apple’s technology built into iOS, on the device.
  • PDF editing, conversions, merging, compression, redaction, signing: everything is computed on the device.
  • Translation: it uses Apple’s translation built into iOS. The languages it needs may be downloaded by iOS from Apple; the app does not send the text of your documents.
  • Face ID: it is handled by iOS. The app only receives the answer “unlocked” or “not unlocked”, never your face.
  • Reading receipts, business cards and ID documents: the recognized information stays in the app.

3. The permissions the app asks for

The app only asks for them when you use the feature concerned. You can withdraw them at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera: to scan your documents.
  • Photos: to import images, and to save a document to your photo library if you ask.
  • Face ID: to protect the app and your private folders.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to dictate a document’s name and tags. The transcription happens on the device when iOS has the language model there; otherwise, iOS may send the dictation recording to Apple to transcribe it, under Apple’s rules.
  • Reminders and Calendar: to create a reminder or an event linked to a document, only when you ask.
  • Contacts: to save the contact created from a business card, only when you ask.
  • Notifications: if you accept, to remind you before a free trial ends. These notifications are scheduled on the device.

4. What can leave your device, when you choose

  • Sharing: when you share, print or send a document, it goes to the app or service you choose.
  • Copy to iCloud Drive: if you turn it on, a copy of your documents is placed in your own iCloud Drive, run by Apple. Documents in your private folders are not copied there.
  • Backup: the archive you export goes wherever you save it.
  • Downloading a font: if a document uses a free font that is missing and you agree to download it, the app gets it from its original site (for example GitHub or CTAN). As with any Internet connection, that site sees the IP address of your connection. No information about your documents is sent. Fonts offered by Apple are downloaded by iOS, from Apple.
  • Links: an address recognized on a document opens in Maps, and a link read from a QR code opens in your browser, only if you tap them.

5. Purchases

The subscriptions and the lifetime purchase of “Retouche PDF Pro” are sold and collected by Apple, on the App Store. We receive neither your name, nor your email address, nor your payment details. The app checks your purchases with Apple, on the device. Apple only sends us sales statistics, without your identity. The number of free uses already spent is kept on your device, in its keychain; it may stay there after you delete the app (see section 7).

6. Crash reports

If you have agreed, in iOS Settings, to share analytics data with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports. These technical reports don’t contain your documents. You can stop this sharing at any time in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements).

7. How long data is kept

Your documents stay on your device until you delete them. A deleted document goes to the app’s Trash: it stays there for 30 days, then it is erased automatically; you can also empty the Trash yourself. Deleting the app erases what it saved on the device, except what you copied elsewhere (iCloud Drive, backup, sharing) and the number of free uses already spent: iOS keeps it in the device’s keychain, and the app finds it again if you reinstall it. This number contains no document and no information about you.

8. Your rights

As we receive no personal data, we hold none about you. You can still write to us with any question at retouchepdf@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

9. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and collects no data, theirs or anyone else’s.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date. This policy also exists in French.
