Scan and edit your PDFs on your iPhone

Retouche PDF scans your documents and edits your PDFs right on your iPhone and iPad. The text you correct is rewritten in the file, in the document’s original font when that font contains the letters you type.

Your documents are processed on your device. No account, no ads, no analytics, and no server of ours: a document leaves your device only if you share it, back it up or copy it to your own iCloud Drive.

Free, with Retouche PDF Pro as an option: a monthly or yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

The app is available in English and French.
